MATERIAL SCIENCE & ARCHITECTURAL CRAFT

Design Notes on Metals & Finishes

Deep dive into the structural alloys and architectural treatments that define ForgeNest residential engineering.

MILD STEEL (MS)

Structural Versatility & Rigidity

Ideal for large-span driveway gates and heavy automated sliders. Provides exceptional impact resistance when fabricated with 12 to 14 gauge heavy-wall tubular box sections.

Best suited for: Villa Entrance Gates & Perimeter Pillars
STAINLESS STEEL (SS 304 / 316)

Corrosion Immunity & Satin Luster

Contains 18% chromium and 8% nickel to form a self-healing passive oxide film. Completely impervious to rain, high humidity, and seasonal temperature cycling.

Best suited for: Terrace Balustrades & Staircase Handrails
ARCHITECTURAL ALUMINIUM

Lightweight Slimline Facade Systems

60% lighter than structural steel, reducing strain on motor automated tracks. Extruded aerodynamic profiles accept rich anodized bronze and matte charcoal finishes.

Best suited for: Window Safety Screens & Louvered Facades
HYBRID ARCHITECTURAL INFILLS

Laminated Glass & Composite Wood

Combining dark metal framework with 12mm toughened laminated glass or weather-proof WPC timber planks creates warmth, privacy, and wind shielding.

Best suited for: Contemporary Facades & Balcony Windbreaks
