MS vs Stainless Steel vs Aluminium: Understanding the Visual Difference

By Metallurgy Insights • 6 min read • Materials Guide

Selecting the ideal metal for your residential gates, balustrades, or grills is a balancing act between tensile strength, architectural weight, surface texture, and long-term climate resilience.

1. Mild Steel (MS): Sculptural Mass & Value

Mild steel remains the global standard for custom architectural gates due to its exceptional rigidity and ease of welding. When combined with heavy gauge tubing and zinc powder coating, it offers decades of structural integrity at an accessible price point.

2. Stainless Steel (SS 304/316): Marine Grade Longevity

Stainless steel incorporates chromium and nickel (plus molybdenum in SS 316) to form a self-healing passive chromium oxide layer. It delivers a refined satin or brushed contemporary luster that requires zero repainting even in humid coastal zones.

3. Architectural Aluminium: Lightweight Modernity

Weighing roughly one-third of steel, extruded 6063-T6 aluminum allows for ultra-wide motorized sliding gates that operate with whisper-quiet motor effort and zero risk of red rust formation.
