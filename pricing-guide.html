COST DRIVERS

Why Project Estimates Vary

Because every residence is custom-measured, pricing is determined by eight practical technical factors.

1. Opening Dimensions

Clear opening width and height determine total steel tonnage and structural framing.

2. Material Grade

Mild Steel vs SS 304/316 alloys vs extruded architectural aluminium profiles.

3. Design Complexity

Straight horizontal louver slats vs intricate laser-cut CNC architectural screen inlays.

4. Finish & Coating

Zinc phosphating, hot-dip galvanizing, and multi-stage architectural powder coats.

5. Hardware & Locks

Heavy-duty ball-bearing hinges, flush lock-boxes, and concealed floor stops.

6. Automation Kits

Motorized sliding gate tracks, safety infrared beams, and smart wireless receivers.

7. Site Conditions

Driveway gradient leveling, pillar anchor load reinforcement, and conduit routing.

8. Installation & Access

On-site precision mounting, crane lifting access, and structural anchor load testing.

CATEGORY BREAKDOWN

Cost Factors by Service

What to consider when budgeting for specific residential fabrication scopes.

Main Entrance Gates

Primary considerations include total width (feet), sliding track vs cantilever mechanism, steel gauge wall thickness, and integrated pedestrian door wickets.

Balustrades & Railings

Driven by running length (rft), glass thickness (10mm/12mm/15mm tempered), stainless steel alloy grade (304 vs marine 316), and mounting spigot hardware.

Window Safety Guards

Influenced by total window opening count, bar spacing (inches on center), decorative collar details, and internal masonry fastener anchors.

DESIGN COMPLEXITY TIERS

Design Directions

Three design directions representing different levels of fabrication detail and materiality.

DIRECTION 01

Essential

Clean linear horizontal or vertical steel tubing with standard powder coating. Focuses on straightforward security and durable simplicity.

DIRECTION 02

Balanced

Enhanced privacy louvers, wood-composite slat inlays, satin stainless steel pull bars, and dual-layer zinc anti-rust primer coatings.

DIRECTION 03

Statement

Bespoke CNC laser-perforated artistic screens, integrated architectural backlighting reveals, warm copper trims, and heavy motorized spans.

TRANSPARENCY STANDARD

What a Quotation Should Explain

A professional fabrication quote should leave zero ambiguities regarding scope, materials, or on-site deliverables.

Detailed Scope

Clear breakdown of each gate leaf, balustrade run, or window grill panel.

Material Grade & Gauge

Exact steel wall thickness (gauge/mm) and alloy specifications (MS, SS 304/316).

Finish & Coating System

Specified zinc phosphating pre-treatment and powder coat brand.

Hardware Inclusions

Ball-bearing hinges, lock-boxes, ground track rails, and stoppers.

Installation & Anchors

On-site delivery, structural chemical anchoring, and clean site cleanup.

Timeline & Warranty

Fabrication schedule, delivery date commitment, and structural warranty terms.
