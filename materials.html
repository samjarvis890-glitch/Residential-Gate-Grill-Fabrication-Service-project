STRUCTURAL VERSATILITY

Mild Steel (MS)

Visual Personality: Solid, grounded, and architectural. Provides bold shadow lines and crisp 90-degree mitred corners.

Ideal For: Main entrance gates, window security guards, and heavy compound boundaries.
COASTAL RESILIENCE

Stainless Steel (SS 304 / 316)

Visual Personality: Sleek, refined, and luminous. Satin brushed textures that maintain luster without maintenance.

Ideal For: Balcony handrails, glass spigot fittings, and seaside residential installations.
FEATHERWEIGHT PRECISION

Architectural Aluminium

Visual Personality: Crisp, ultra-clean, and contemporary. Zero rust risk with flawless powder adhesion.

Ideal For: Extra-wide sliding gates, facade screens, and horizontal privacy louvers.
LIGHT & SIGHTLINES

Glass + Metal Systems

Visual Personality: Weightless and transparent. Maximizes architectural views and natural landscape light.

Ideal For: Terrace balustrades, internal staircases, and modern villa balconies.
