OUR STORY & VISION

Crafting the First Impression of Home

ForgeNest was founded on a simple belief: residential metalwork should not be treated as mere utilitarian welding, but as an enduring architectural element designed around the home.

Book Free Visit View Products
RESIDENTIAL FOCUS

Designed for Homes, Not Industrial Yards

Most fabrication shops operate with an industrial mindset—focused on bulk tonnage rather than residential proportions, smooth weld finishes, and clean on-site installation.

At ForgeNest, every project is residential. We obsess over the tactile details: flush lock-boxes, silent ball-bearing gate hinges, clean glass balustrade spigots, and powder coat textures that complement natural stone and timber facade elements.

“We build security elements that look like they were always meant to be part of the architecture.”
CONSULTATIVE METHOD

Our 5-Step Approach

How we guide homeowners from early visual inspiration to flawless handover.

01

Understand

Listening to your home’s privacy needs, aesthetic preferences, and budget expectations.

02

Design

Drafting CAD elevation models and slat proportion studies for clear architectural alignment.

03

Material

Selecting the appropriate alloy (MS, SS 304/316, or Aluminium) and protective finish system.

04

Measure

Laser on-site survey recording exact wall plumb lines, ground elevations, and structural points.

05

Plan & Fit

Careful workshop fabrication, protective packaging, and meticulous on-site installation.

METALLURGICAL QUALITY

Craftsmanship & Material Thinking

Metalwork quality is defined by what happens before the final coat of paint is applied. Structural weld penetration, chemical rust de-scaling, and zinc phosphate undercoats form the foundation of durability.

Construction Quality: Heavy-duty box sections and precision mitred corner joints.
Surface Finishing: Architectural electrostatic powder coating cured at 200°C.
Material Choice: Environment-specific alloy selection for urban and coastal residences.
